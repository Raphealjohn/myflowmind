Frequently Asked Questions

  • How long does a typical project take?
    Most Starter projects are live within two weeks. Growth projects typically take three weeks from the kickoff call to go-live. Enterprise timelines are scoped individually but we always aim to deliver value early through a phased approach.
  • Do I need technical knowledge to use the automations you build?
    No. We build every system with non-technical users in mind. We provide video walkthroughs, written documentation, and a live training session so your team can operate and understand the automations without any coding knowledge.
  • What tools and platforms do you work with?
    We work with the full ecosystem: Make.com, Zapier, n8n, HubSpot, Salesforce, Airtable, Notion, Google Workspace, Slack, and many more. We also build custom API integrations for tools with no off-the-shelf connector.
  • What happens if an automation breaks after launch?
    All plans include a bug fix guarantee — 30 days on Starter, 60 days on Growth. If a workflow breaks due to our implementation, we fix it at no charge. We also offer ongoing maintenance retainers for clients who want long-term peace of mind.
  • Is my data safe when you build these automations?
    Absolutely. We follow data minimisation principles, never store client data beyond what is necessary for building the system, use encrypted connections throughout, and can sign NDAs and DPAs on request. All AI integrations are configured with privacy settings that prevent your data from being used for model training.
