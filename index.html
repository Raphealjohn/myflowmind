Hi, I'm Rapheal John — Founder & CEO of MyFlowMind, an AI automation studio based in Des Moines, Iowa.

With a background spanning cybersecurity, systems integration, and applied AI, I started MyFlowMind after watching talented teams lose thousands of hours a year to manual, repetitive work. I knew there was a better way — and I set out to build it.
